Current Target

Orion Nebula (M42) • मृगशीर्ष

Emission Nebula 1,344 Light-Years Nakshatra: Mrigashirsha

A massive cosmic stellar nursery where baby stars are born inside swirling clouds of energized ionized hydrogen gas and stellar dust. In Indian lore, this constellation represents Mriga (the Celestial Deer).

What you will view in person:

Silky bioluminescent wisps surrounding the Trapezium quadruple star system, glowing distinctly against Chiplun's dark mountain sky.

Indian Celestial Heritage

The 27 Nakshatras (नक्षत्र)

The ecliptic is divided into 27 lunar segments. Explore the major Nakshatras visible in our winter and spring sessions at Chiplun.

Why the name 'Punarvasu'?

पुनर्वसु: The Return of the Light & Renewal

Punarvasu literally translates to "Good Again" or "Return of the Auspicious Light". Governed by Aditi (the boundless cosmic mother), its marker stars are Castor and Pollux in Gemini. It represents a renewal of hope, clear vision, and homecoming—the ideal patron star for our Terav sanctuary.
